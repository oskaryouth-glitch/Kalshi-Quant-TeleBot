Reading it

  • Cash risk on a small locker is modest. With a borrowed SUV and a light load, a $100 winning bid breaks even in cash at roughly $180 of resale.
  • Your time decides the answer. Selling is about 80% of the labor: roughly 44 minutes per sale in this model. At a $25 average sale, paying yourself $40 an hour never breaks even.
  • Heavy disposal hurts the 5x10 far more than the 5x5, through dump weight, mattresses and extra trips.
  • None of this says lockers contain this much value. Only real purchases can show that.
Assumptions: 18% buyer premium (StorageTreasures Basic), licensed reseller buying tax-exempt and remitting 8.2% on local sales (75% local, 25% eBay at 13.6% + $0.40), Woodmen Dump weight pricing, U-Haul 18+ / Home Depot 21+ rentals, $100 cleaning deposit, 10-mile trips, 15 min per listing and 25 min per sale.
